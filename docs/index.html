NCURA Region VI/VII Regional Meeting, Denver

Augmenting Research Administration with AI

The value of any AI tool is bounded by the quality of the data underneath it, and by the nature of the work itself. This three-hour, hands-on workshop builds a practical understanding of where AI can support research administration and where it cannot, with open-source AI4RA tools and a structured ethical discussion you can take home.

Where NCURA RM26, Denver, Colorado. Room TBA.
Presenters Nathan Layman and Bert Baumgaertner
Format Three hours. Ninety minutes with Nathan, an hour with Bert, and time to talk.

What you will leave with

Foundational skills to use AI responsibly in your own workflows while keeping human oversight.

Understand

How AI works, and where it fails

How large language models generate output, the tasks they perform well, and the ways they fail. Which research administration activities benefit from AI, which depend on data infrastructure, and which should stay human-driven.

Practice

Hands-on with open-source AI4RA tools

A prompt engineering trainer, a document extraction and review workflow, and a data lakehouse architecture for preparing institutional data. Leave with a mental model for evaluating output and judging data readiness.

Govern

Ethics, judgment, and oversight

A structured ethical discussion mini-workshop you can run at your own institution, plus reasoning puzzles that show where human judgment still beats the machine and what verification and oversight should look like.

Before you arrive

Bring a laptop and one task from your own work that you suspect AI could help with.

The activities are designed around real research administration tasks. The more concrete your example, the more useful the workshop will be.

The hands-on activities use Promptulus, which needs an API key. For this workshop the University of Idaho provides one through MindRouter, so you do not need your own. Scan the code or open the short link and the key is loaded for you.

How is the key handled?
  • Tab-scoped storage. The key is held in sessionStorage, which is scoped to this browser tab. It clears when you close the tab and is never written to disk or shared across tabs.
  • Static site. This page is served as a static file from GitHub Pages. No server here stores or sees your key.
  • Passed to Promptulus at click time. Links to Promptulus from this site carry the key once, and Promptulus removes it from its own address bar on load.
  • Sent only to MindRouter. A key starting with mr2_ routes requests to the University of Idaho's models. Nothing goes to a public AI vendor on your account.
  • URL stripped. If the key arrives via ?key=, it is read once and immediately removed from the address bar so it does not land in history or bookmarks.
  • Workshop-scoped. The key is issued for this event and retired afterward. It is never stored in this site's source. A personal Gemini key pasted above works too.

Note: as with any client-side application, the key is visible in browser developer tools while the page is open. Do not paste a key attached to a paid production project.

Facilitators: pre-fill by sharing a link to this site with ?key=YOUR_KEY appended. The short link and QR code do exactly that.
