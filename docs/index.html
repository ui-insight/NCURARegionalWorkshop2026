NCURA Region VI/VII Regional Meeting, Denver

Augmenting Research Administration with AI

A three-hour, hands-on workshop that builds foundational AI literacy for research administrators. Participants learn how generative and agentic AI actually work, practice designing and evaluating prompts, and work through the governance questions that come with AI-assisted workflows.

View the sessions Meet the presenters

Materials on this site will grow as the workshop takes shape. Check back before Denver for slides, activities, and resources.

Funded by National Science Foundation grant #2427549

When Sunday, November 8, 2026
Where NCURA RM26, Denver, Colorado. Room TBA.
Presenters Nathan Layman and Bert Baumgaertner
Format Three hours. Three modules. Interactive throughout.

What you will leave with

Foundational skills to use AI responsibly in your own workflows while keeping human oversight.

Understand

How AI works, and where it fails

Clear, jargon-free explanations of generative models, agentic AI, and workflow automation, including their limitations and the complementary role they can play in research administration.

Practice

Prompting, decomposition, and evaluation

Hands-on practice selecting an appropriate model, breaking a task into manageable pieces, writing high-quality prompts, and checking outputs for accuracy before they enter a workflow.

Govern

Policy, risk, and accountability

Interactive discussion of transparency, accountability, and reproducibility, applied to institutional policies and real-world scenarios from your own office.

Before you arrive

Bring a laptop and one task from your own work that you suspect AI could help with.

The activities are designed around real research administration tasks. The more concrete your example, the more useful the workshop will be.
