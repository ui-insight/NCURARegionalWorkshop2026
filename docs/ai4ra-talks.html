AI4RA talks

More from the AI4RA team at the Denver meeting.

After the Sunday workshop, people from the AI4RA team at the University of Idaho and Southern Utah University are presenting throughout the week. Times and rooms come from the preliminary program updated October 26, 2026. Check the official program in case anything moves.

Monday, November 9

2:45 PM · Lawrence B

Roping In Trustworthy Results from AI Tools

Sarah Martonick, University of Idaho, with Julie Wenzel, Colorado State University

An interactive session on using AI as a reliable partner in daily work: clear prompts, workflows you can run again, and breaking big, messy tasks into pieces an AI can handle.

Tuesday, November 10

10:30 AM · Lawrence A

High Noon for AI Adoption: Draw Up Your Plan

Sarah Martonick, University of Idaho

What it takes to bring a tool like Vandalizer into daily work at the enterprise level: why OCR and model choice matter, how to check outputs for consistency and accuracy, and a starter kit for deploying an on-premises AI tool securely.

Vandalizer
2:45 PM · Confluence C

Taming the Endless To-Do Lists: Strategies for Wrangling Compliance Issues in Grant Financial Accounts

Courtney Wessling, Nathan Wiggins and Carrie Childs, Southern Utah University

Simpler grant budget and account monitoring, with strategies you can put in place quickly to spot high-risk situations and catch compliance problems as they happen.

4:00 PM · Platte

The Good, the Bad, and the Ungoverned: Evaluating AI Outputs at Scale

Barrie Robison, University of Idaho

How to evaluate AI outputs across many documents, prompts and tools, and over time. An emerging AI4RA framework for evaluation evidence, with examples from award document extraction and federal RFA analysis. Covers the prompt library and the evaluation harness behind it.

Prompt library Evaluation harness

Wednesday, November 11

9:00 AM · Lawrence B

PROSPER Beyond the 8 Second Ride in a Dept/PUI: Saddling Staff for Success

Sylvia Bradshaw, Southern Utah University

How Southern Utah University grew its PUI research office to a thirteen-person team through new staffing models developed under the NSF GRANTED program, and how it plans to sustain them.

10:45 AM · Platte

Your Risk-Averse Mindset Needed: Responsibly Navigating the AI Adoption Rodeo

Nathan Wiggins, Southern Utah University, with Jessica Robins, Arizona State University

How data moves through AI systems, and what institutions can do to use AI responsibly while protecting institutional data, so you can decide when and how AI is safe to use in your own setting.
