NARC: Narrative Augmented Reasoning Challenges

Bert Baumgaertner's puzzle platform. Each puzzle pairs a sequence of colored grids with a short narrative clue that only helps when read figuratively. Neither alone is enough; together they determine the answer.

Discussion instrument: Research Administration and AI

Sixteen statements, a facilitator guide, and a printable participant sheet for running a structured conversation about AI at your own institution. Free to use and adapt under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Best with a facilitator trained in ethics, philosophy, or a related field.

The shared schema for research administration data: forty tables across proposals, awards, finance, effort, and compliance. Map each of your systems to it once and any tool built on it runs at your institution.
